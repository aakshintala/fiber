Mockup for the #4280 settings design. It's interactive: click the terminal, then use ←→ to change tab, ↑↓ to move, Enter or Space to change a value, Tab to move a value to another layer, Delete to remove an override, and / or typing to search. Esc clears the search. The values and layers are made up. Not to scale: rows are spaced at 1.5 lines to show the readability spacing.
