# Images and links (#1637)

One run per case: `--image-case CASE`.

- tool_image: the read ledger row with its blue tick and dimensions, the image below it through kitty placeholders where the terminal speaks the protocol and its one line everywhere else.
- fallback: the one-line image row in the link look, and a second row for an image that cannot show with the notice saying why.
- pasted: the prompt bubble with [Image #1] and the image through kitty placeholders where the terminal speaks the protocol and its one line everywhere else.
- links: the reply with its focused and hovered links, the table with its focused and hovered link cells, the focused link on the selection tint with the Enter-opens hint.
- screen_reader: the flat frame with no tints, edges, stripes or animation: one line per item in reading order, state in words not glyphs, the image as its one line.
- title_approval: the labelled OSC 2 row for `! fiber · approval`, written to the terminal and restored to `fiber` on exit.
- title_question: the labelled OSC 2 row for `! fiber · question`, written to the terminal and restored to `fiber` on exit.
- title_waiting: the labelled OSC 2 row for `! fiber · waiting`, written to the terminal and restored to `fiber` on exit.
- title_finished: the labelled OSC 2 row for `✓ fiber · finished`, written to the terminal and restored to `fiber` on exit.
