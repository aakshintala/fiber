# Repository offer and one-question forms (#1636)

One run per case: `--offer-case CASE`.

- offer: the extension, the hook and the MCP server, each with what an install shows and three choices starting at skip, the changed server's diff with its red removal, the TUI-files sentence, and the choose/send/put-aside hint.
- confirm: the approval slab with its stripe, the title with Esc right-aligned and no tab row, the question, yes and no, and Chat about this last with no Submit row.
- select: the options with their descriptions and ( )/(•) marks under the cursor, and Chat about this last with no Submit row.
- multi_select: choose any, the [ ]/[x] marks, the Submit row, then Chat about this.
- text_input: only the answer-in-words row with its cursor, the Submit row, then Chat about this.
