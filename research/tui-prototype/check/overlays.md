# Overlays (#1630)

One run per case: `--overlay CASE`.

- keymap: the key map should float centred over the dimmed conversation with even ▄ ▀ edges, a bold `Key map` title with ✕ at its right end and a dim `esc closes` foot; every binding grouped by area in two aligned columns, each with its action, key, other paths and id, nothing clipped.
- keymap-narrow: the same content in one column, opened scrolled, with an `↑ N more · ↓ M more` indicator on its last line.
- quit: the question should read `2 sessions working` with `enter`, `c` and `esc` naming the three ways out.
- delete: the question should name `docs: rail spec` and its spend, and what `--cascade` would add.
- history: the typed `back` should read bold after `›`, every hit in the three matches marked, the first row marked with ▌ on the lighter tint.
- notice: the whole `key_clash` text should read wrapped to the overlay, nothing clipped.
- close-mouse: the ✕ should read lighter than its neighbours, the foot should read `click ✕ or outside to close`, and a dim dotted outline should mark the click-outside target.
