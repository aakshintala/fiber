## Method notes
Composition and State were the productive dimensions: tracing every `admit` caller against the credential gate found the P2. Tracing the warm and cold cache paths separately settled the double-emission worry, both in the session (detached refresh) and in the TUI (text dedupe). Limits, Resources and Environment were mostly padding on a filter of this size, though Environment did make me check the hub and package version skew the ticket hints at ("marks it does not understand") and rule it out through docs. The "one more" step was real here: the question "who sees a notice for a provider that is off?" sat across Composition and Contract and gave the only finding. Concurrency only gave a non-finding, because the TUI dedupes notices by their text.
