## Method notes
Composition, Contract and the "one more" step were productive. "One more" (definition purity) led straight to the P1, and Composition led to the hub bypass. Input and Environment felt like padding: serde and cfg already cover them. Concurrency was quick to dismiss because everything runs on the loop thread. Failure added nothing. The useful move was tracing *when* a changed pure-looking function is evaluated, which no dimension asks for explicitly. I suggest adding it to the list as "evaluation time / caching of changed values".
