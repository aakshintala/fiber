## Method notes
Wiring and concurrency did the most work. The reach step showed that most of the semantics (deny steps, depth, self-call, cancel, ran_by) live in the loop crate from #555, so here they could only be traced, not attacked. Environment and resources were padding on this change. The "one more" item (a user coroutine re-yielding a host call and mutating the shared table) found nothing, because the authors had already guarded it. That suggests the list could carry "a value aliased across a yield" as a standing item. The empty-object ambiguity came from the Input dimension, not from the "one more".
